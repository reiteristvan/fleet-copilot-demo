# Service report — SD50B-2026-10142 — 2026-06-03

## Findings

Called to Metro Logistics Centre. Reported symptom was poor water pickup on SD50B-2026-10142. Found the squeegee deflection uneven across the width and the left hand blade worn well past its limit.

## Work carried out

1. Updated the charger profile to the setting for the fitted pack chemistry.
2. Recalibrated the deck height and confirmed even contact across the width.
3. Replaced the panel filter and its seal; the old seal had taken a compression set.

## Parts used

- No parts used; adjustment only
- Brush set, both sides

## Outcome

Returned to service. The underlying cause is operational rather than mechanical: the machine is being put on charge hot at the end of a double shift.
