# Ride-on scrubber-dryer SDR-90 — service manual

## Scope

For service technicians working on the Ride-on scrubber-dryer SDR-90. Every torque figure in this document is for clean, dry threads unless stated otherwise.

## Service intervals

| Interval | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Rinse both tanks, check squeegee blades, record hour meter |
| 50 h | Measure brush bristle length against the wear limit |
| 250 h | Inspect filter and seal, check deck height across the width |
| 500 h | Full battery capacity check and charger profile verification |
| 1000 h | Replace squeegee blade set and drive plate regardless of wear |

## Diagnostics

1. Reproduce the fault with the machine on the floor, not on stands; load changes the symptom.
2. Verify the float shut-off operates freely before investigating a vacuum complaint.
3. Check the battery state of charge before diagnosing any intermittent electrical fault.
4. Check for wound debris on the brush drive before condemning the motor.
5. Confirm the reported symptom yourself rather than working from the handover note.
6. Compare the hour meter against the service history to see whether an interval was missed.

## Parts and consumables

- Filter seals are consumables, not hardware. Replace the seal whenever the filter is replaced.
- Battery packs are matched sets. Replacing one cell block in an aged pack loads the new block against the old ones and shortens the life of both.
- Order consumables against the item number, since two variants of one machine type differ.
- Brush and pad driver part numbers differ between the pad deck variants. Check the item number on the machine plate, not the machine type.

## Safety

- Isolate the battery before any work on the traction or brush circuits.
- Support a raised deck mechanically. The lift mechanism is not a hold device.
- Wear eye protection when working near a charging battery.
