# Walk-behind scrubber-dryer SD-50B — operator manual

## Intended use

The Walk-behind scrubber-dryer SD-50B is intended for cleaning sealed hard floors indoors. It is not approved for outdoor use, for floors with a gradient above the value on the machine plate, or for picking up burning, explosive or toxic material.

## Controls

- The hour meter records running time and is the basis for every service interval.
- The battery indicator shows the state of charge of the AGM pack.
- The deck lever raises and lowers the pad deck. Raise it before reversing.
- The key switch isolates the whole machine. Remove the key whenever the machine is unattended.
- The emergency stop cuts traction and brush drive but does not isolate the battery.

## Daily operation

1. Overlap each pass by about one tenth of the deck width.
2. Check the battery indicator shows enough charge for the whole run.
3. Watch behind the machine: water on the floor means the squeegee is not picking up.
4. Select a speed that keeps the squeegee picking up cleanly; faster is not cleaner.
5. Lower the deck and open the solution tap.
6. Walk the area and remove loose debris and obstacles before starting.

## Filling and emptying

1. Add detergent at the dilution on the product label, into water, never the other way round.
2. Rinse the recovery tank at the end of every shift and leave the lid open to dry.
3. Empty the recovery tank before it reaches full, not after the shut-off has tripped.
4. Do not exceed the 40 litre mark. The recovery tank holds 45 litres and must be able to take back everything put down.

## After use

1. Put the machine on charge and confirm the charger has started.
2. Remove the brush or pad driver and rinse it.
3. Record the hour meter reading and anything unusual in the machine log.
4. Wipe the squeegee blades and check them for damage.
5. Raise the squeegee off the floor so the blades do not take a set overnight.

## Cautions

- Report any fault code that recurs, even if it clears itself.
- Do not hose the machine down. Water entering the electrical compartment is not covered.
- Replace the squeegee blade once the wiping edge is worn back more than 3 mm. A worn blade leaves a wet floor, which is a slip hazard rather than a cosmetic problem.
- Replace brushes at or below 12 mm bristle length. Past that point the deck carries load the bristles should take.
