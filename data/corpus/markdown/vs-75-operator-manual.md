# Vacuum sweeper VS-75 — operator manual

## Intended use

The Vacuum sweeper VS-75 is intended for cleaning sealed hard floors indoors. It is not approved for outdoor use, for floors with a gradient above the value on the machine plate, or for picking up burning, explosive or toxic material.

## Configurations covered

| Item number | Battery | Deck | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.688-420.0 | AGM | brush | 50 l hopper |
| 1.688-421.0 | lithium-ion | brush | 50 l hopper |

## Controls

- The hour meter records running time and is the basis for every service interval.
- The deck lever raises and lowers the brush deck. Raise it before reversing.
- The key switch isolates the whole machine. Remove the key whenever the machine is unattended.
- The emergency stop cuts traction and brush drive but does not isolate the battery.
- The battery indicator shows the state of charge of the AGM pack.

## Daily operation

1. Walk the area and remove loose debris and obstacles before starting.
2. Overlap each pass by about one tenth of the deck width.
3. Check the battery indicator shows enough charge for the whole run.
4. Select a speed that keeps the squeegee picking up cleanly; faster is not cleaner.
5. Lower the deck and open the solution tap.
6. Watch behind the machine: water on the floor means the squeegee is not picking up.

## Filling and emptying

1. Rinse the recovery tank at the end of every shift and leave the lid open to dry.
2. Add detergent at the dilution on the product label, into water, never the other way round.
3. Empty the recovery tank before it reaches full, not after the shut-off has tripped.
4. Empty the 50 litre hopper at the end of every shift, running the filter shaker first.

## After use

1. Raise the squeegee off the floor so the blades do not take a set overnight.
2. Wipe the squeegee blades and check them for damage.
3. Drain and rinse both tanks.
4. Remove the brush or pad driver and rinse it.
5. Put the machine on charge and confirm the charger has started.

## Cautions

- Report any fault code that recurs, even if it clears itself.
- Do not hose the machine down. Water entering the electrical compartment is not covered.
- Replace brushes at or below 12 mm bristle length. Past that point the deck carries load the bristles should take.
- Never open the battery compartment while the charger is connected.
