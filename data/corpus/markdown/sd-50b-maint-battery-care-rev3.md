# Battery care — Walk-behind scrubber-dryer SD-50B

## Purpose

For the Walk-behind scrubber-dryer SD-50B, item number 1.512-340.0. Read the whole procedure before starting: two of the steps cannot be undone once begun.

## Tools required

- Vernier caliper or the wear gauge supplied with the machine
- Torque wrench, 5-25 Nm
- Nitrile gloves
- 13 mm combination spanner

## Procedure

1. Park the machine in a ventilated area away from ignition sources.
2. Switch off at the key and wait two minutes for the traction circuit to discharge.
3. Check the pack surface temperature before connecting the charger.
4. Do not begin charging if the pack surface temperature is above 45 C. Allow it to cool first; charging a hot pack is what produces the over-temperature lockout, not the other way round.
5. Connect the charger and confirm the display shows a charge current within the first minute.
6. Leave the pack on charge until the charger signals complete. Interrupting a cycle and resuming later is not equivalent to one full cycle.
7. Check that no cell is more than 0.2 V from the pack average once charging finishes.
8. Record the end-of-charge voltage in the machine log.
9. Never leave a pack below its minimum state of charge overnight. A deep discharge is not visible until the pack is next load-tested.

## Limits and intervals

| Item | Limit | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squeegee blade | 3 mm wear | Check daily |
| Brush bristle | 12 mm minimum length | Measure every 50 h |
| Charging temperature | 45 C maximum | Before every charge |
| Filter seal | Replaces with filter | Every 250 h |

## Notes

- Dispose of used consumables according to the site waste procedure. Detergent-soaked material is not general waste.
- Record the hour meter reading in the machine log when the procedure is complete. The next interval is counted from that reading, not from the calendar date.
