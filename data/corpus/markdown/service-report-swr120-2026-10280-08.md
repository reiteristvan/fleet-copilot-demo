# Service report — SWR120-2026-10280 — 2026-06-24

## Findings

Inspection of SWR120-2026-10280 found the recovery tank float shut-off coated with detergent residue and sticking, which accounts for the intermittent E-088.

## Work carried out

1. Advised the site on the charging temperature limit and left a copy of the procedure.
2. Replaced the panel filter and its seal; the old seal had taken a compression set.
3. Cleared the fault history after recording it, then ran the machine for twenty minutes.

## Parts used

- Panel filter and seal
- Brush set, both sides

## Outcome

Fault not reproduced after the adjustment. Asked the site to log it again if it returns.
