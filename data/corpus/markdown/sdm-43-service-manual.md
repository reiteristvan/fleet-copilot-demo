# Single-disc machine SDM-43 — service manual

## Scope

For service technicians working on the Single-disc machine SDM-43. Every torque figure in this document is for clean, dry threads unless stated otherwise.

## Service intervals

| Interval | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Rinse both tanks, check squeegee blades, record hour meter |
| 50 h | Measure brush bristle length against the wear limit |
| 250 h | Inspect filter and seal, check deck height across the width |
| 500 h | Full battery capacity check and charger profile verification |
| 1000 h | Replace squeegee blade set and drive plate regardless of wear |

## Diagnostics

1. Verify the float shut-off operates freely before investigating a vacuum complaint.
2. Read and record the fault history before clearing anything; clearing it discards the evidence.
3. Measure at the connector, not at the component, when isolating a wiring fault.
4. Confirm the reported symptom yourself rather than working from the handover note.
5. Compare the hour meter against the service history to see whether an interval was missed.
6. Check the battery state of charge before diagnosing any intermittent electrical fault.

## Parts and consumables

- Order consumables against the item number, since two variants of one machine type differ.
- Filter seals are consumables, not hardware. Replace the seal whenever the filter is replaced.
- Squeegee blades are supplied as a set of four edges. Fitting a single replacement edge against three worn ones produces uneven deflection, and the complaint returns within the week.
- Battery packs are matched sets. Replacing one cell block in an aged pack loads the new block against the old ones and shortens the life of both.

## Safety

- Wear eye protection when working near a charging battery.
- The emergency stop is not an isolator. It cuts drive but leaves the battery connected, and treating it as an isolator is how technicians are injured on these machines.
- Support a raised deck mechanically. The lift mechanism is not a hold device.
