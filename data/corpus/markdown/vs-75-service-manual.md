# Vacuum sweeper VS-75 — service manual

## Scope

This manual covers corrective and preventive service on the Vacuum sweeper VS-75. It assumes the technician is qualified to work on battery systems and has the machine isolated before starting.

## Service intervals

| Interval | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Rinse both tanks, check squeegee blades, record hour meter |
| 50 h | Measure brush bristle length against the wear limit |
| 250 h | Inspect filter and seal, check deck height across the width |
| 500 h | Full battery capacity check and charger profile verification |
| 1000 h | Replace squeegee blade set and drive plate regardless of wear |

## Diagnostics

1. Measure at the connector, not at the component, when isolating a wiring fault.
2. Check the battery state of charge before diagnosing any intermittent electrical fault.
3. Verify the float shut-off operates freely before investigating a vacuum complaint.
4. Compare the hour meter against the service history to see whether an interval was missed.
5. Reproduce the fault with the machine on the floor, not on stands; load changes the symptom.
6. Confirm the reported symptom yourself rather than working from the handover note.

## Parts and consumables

- Battery packs are matched sets. Replacing one cell block in an aged pack loads the new block against the old ones and shortens the life of both.
- Squeegee blades are supplied as a set of four edges. Fitting a single replacement edge against three worn ones produces uneven deflection, and the complaint returns within the week.
- Brush and pad driver part numbers differ between the brush deck variants. Check the item number on the machine plate, not the machine type.
- Filter seals are consumables, not hardware. Replace the seal whenever the filter is replaced.

## Safety

- Isolate the battery before any work on the traction or brush circuits.
- Wear eye protection when working near a charging battery.
- The emergency stop is not an isolator. It cuts drive but leaves the battery connected, and treating it as an isolator is how technicians are injured on these machines.
