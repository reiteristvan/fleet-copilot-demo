# Brush wear limits — Ride-on sweeper SWR-120

## Purpose

This procedure covers the Ride-on sweeper SWR-120 and applies to item number 1.721-106.0. Carry it out at the interval in the table below, or sooner if the daily check finds a fault.

## Tools required

- Torque wrench, 5-25 Nm
- Vernier caliper or the wear gauge supplied with the machine
- Nitrile gloves
- Drain hose

## Procedure

1. Switch the machine off and raise the brush deck.
2. Release the brush from the drive plate by turning it against the direction of travel.
3. Clean the bristles of wound debris before measuring; wrapped film reads as bristle length.
4. Measure the bristle length with the caliper at four points around the brush. Use the shortest reading.
5. Replace the brush if the shortest reading is at or below 12 mm. A brush at the limit still cleans, but the deck begins to carry load the bristles should be taking.
6. Check the drive plate for wear at the same time and record its condition.
7. Refit the brush and confirm it locks positively onto the drive plate.
8. Lower the deck and run for thirty seconds, listening for uneven contact.

## Limits and intervals

| Item | Limit | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squeegee blade | 3 mm wear | Check daily |
| Brush bristle | 12 mm minimum length | Measure every 50 h |
| Charging temperature | 40 C maximum | Before every charge |
| Filter seal | Replaces with filter | Every 250 h |

## Notes

- If a part is at or past its wear limit, replace it now rather than at the next interval. The limit is set where the consumable stops protecting the component behind it, and that component is the expensive one.
- Record the hour meter reading in the machine log when the procedure is complete. The next interval is counted from that reading, not from the calendar date.
