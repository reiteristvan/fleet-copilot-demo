# Ride-on scrubber-dryer SDR-90 — operator manual

## Intended use

The Ride-on scrubber-dryer SDR-90 is a battery machine for indoor hard-floor cleaning. Do not use it to pick up liquids other than cleaning solution, and never use it to recover flammable liquids.

## Configurations covered

| Item number | Battery | Deck | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.534-210.0 | AGM | brush | 110 l solution / 120 l recovery |
| 1.534-211.0 | lithium-ion | brush | 110 l solution / 120 l recovery |
| 1.534-212.0 | lithium-ion | pad | 150 l solution / 160 l recovery |

## Controls

- The deck lever raises and lowers the pad deck. Raise it before reversing.
- The solution tap sets the flow to the deck. Closing it stops flow but does not stop the brushes.
- The emergency stop cuts traction and brush drive but does not isolate the battery.
- The battery indicator shows the state of charge of the lithium-ion pack.
- The key switch isolates the whole machine. Remove the key whenever the machine is unattended.

## Daily operation

1. Walk the area and remove loose debris and obstacles before starting.
2. Overlap each pass by about one tenth of the deck width.
3. Watch behind the machine: water on the floor means the squeegee is not picking up.
4. Raise the deck and close the tap before stopping or reversing.
5. Check the battery indicator shows enough charge for the whole run.
6. Select a speed that keeps the squeegee picking up cleanly; faster is not cleaner.

## Filling and emptying

1. Fill the solution tank to 150 litres with clean water below 50 C. Hotter water shortens tank and seal life without cleaning better.
2. Add detergent at the dilution on the product label, into water, never the other way round.
3. Empty the recovery tank before it reaches full, not after the shut-off has tripped.
4. Rinse the recovery tank at the end of every shift and leave the lid open to dry.

## After use

1. Drain and rinse both tanks.
2. Record the hour meter reading and anything unusual in the machine log.
3. Wipe the squeegee blades and check them for damage.
4. Raise the squeegee off the floor so the blades do not take a set overnight.
5. Remove the brush or pad driver and rinse it.

## Cautions

- Never open the battery compartment while the charger is connected.
- Do not hose the machine down. Water entering the electrical compartment is not covered.
- Replace brushes at or below 12 mm bristle length. Past that point the deck carries load the bristles should take.
- Replace the squeegee blade once the wiping edge is worn back more than 3 mm. A worn blade leaves a wet floor, which is a slip hazard rather than a cosmetic problem.
