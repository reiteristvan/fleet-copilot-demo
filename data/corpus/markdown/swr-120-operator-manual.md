# Ride-on sweeper SWR-120 — operator manual

## Intended use

This manual covers the Ride-on sweeper SWR-120, item number 1.721-106.0. The machine is designed for commercial indoor use on sealed floors. Any other use is outside its approval and voids the warranty.

## Configurations covered

| Item number | Battery | Deck | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.721-105.0 | AGM | brush | 60 l hopper |
| 1.721-106.0 | lithium-ion | brush | 60 l hopper |
| 1.721-107.0 | lithium-ion | brush | 80 l hopper |

## Controls

- The key switch isolates the whole machine. Remove the key whenever the machine is unattended.
- The emergency stop cuts traction and brush drive but does not isolate the battery.
- The solution tap sets the flow to the deck. Closing it stops flow but does not stop the brushes.
- The battery indicator shows the state of charge of the lithium-ion pack.
- The deck lever raises and lowers the brush deck. Raise it before reversing.

## Daily operation

1. Watch behind the machine: water on the floor means the squeegee is not picking up.
2. Overlap each pass by about one tenth of the deck width.
3. Select a speed that keeps the squeegee picking up cleanly; faster is not cleaner.
4. Walk the area and remove loose debris and obstacles before starting.
5. Check the battery indicator shows enough charge for the whole run.
6. Lower the deck and open the solution tap.

## Filling and emptying

1. Empty the 60 litre hopper at the end of every shift, running the filter shaker first.
2. Rinse the recovery tank at the end of every shift and leave the lid open to dry.
3. Add detergent at the dilution on the product label, into water, never the other way round.
4. Empty the recovery tank before it reaches full, not after the shut-off has tripped.

## After use

1. Raise the squeegee off the floor so the blades do not take a set overnight.
2. Drain and rinse both tanks.
3. Wipe the squeegee blades and check them for damage.
4. Remove the brush or pad driver and rinse it.
5. Record the hour meter reading and anything unusual in the machine log.

## Cautions

- Replace brushes at or below 12 mm bristle length. Past that point the deck carries load the bristles should take.
- Report any fault code that recurs, even if it clears itself.
- Replace the squeegee blade once the wiping edge is worn back more than 3 mm. A worn blade leaves a wet floor, which is a slip hazard rather than a cosmetic problem.
- Do not hose the machine down. Water entering the electrical compartment is not covered.
