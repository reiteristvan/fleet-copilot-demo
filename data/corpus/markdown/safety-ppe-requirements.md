# Personal protective equipment

## Hazard

A ride-on machine has a blind area behind and to the offside. On a site with pedestrian traffic this is the hazard that actually causes injuries, not the cleaning chemicals.

## Control measures

- Let the pack cool to below the charging temperature limit before connecting the charger.
- Keep the charging area clear of ignition sources, including battery-powered tools.
- Decant concentrate over a bund or a drip tray, never over an open floor.
- Barrier or sign the wet area behind the machine until it is dry.
- Charge only in the designated bay, with the ventilation running.

## Personal protective equipment

- Eye protection when decanting concentrate or working near a charging pack.
- Nitrile gloves when handling concentrate or working on a battery.
- A face shield rather than glasses when breaking into a pressurised solution line.

## In an emergency

- Machine strike or near miss: stop the machine, isolate it, and report before continuing. A near miss on a ride-on machine is reportable.
- Eye contact: irrigate with running water for at least fifteen minutes, holding the eyelids open, and seek medical attention regardless of how the eye feels afterwards.
- Spill of concentrate: contain with the spill kit, do not flush to drain, and report the volume. Diluted solution may be flushed; concentrate may not.
