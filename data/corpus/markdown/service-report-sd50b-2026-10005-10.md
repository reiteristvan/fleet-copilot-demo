# Service report — SD50B-2026-10005 — 2026-07-10

## Findings

Found shrink wrap wound tightly around the brush drive shaft on SD50B-2026-10005. Bristle length measured within limits once the film was removed, so the overload was mechanical rather than wear.

## Work carried out

1. Replaced the panel filter and its seal; the old seal had taken a compression set.
2. Cleaned the float shut-off and the recovery tank breather, then flushed the suction path.
3. Replaced the squeegee blade set and reset the deflection across the full width.

## Parts used

- Float shut-off assembly
- Drive plate

## Outcome

Left out of service pending a replacement pack. The site has been told not to return it to the floor.
