# Filter cleaning — Ride-on sweeper SWR-120

## Purpose

Applies to the Ride-on sweeper SWR-120. The interval assumes single-shift use on a sealed floor; halve it for two-shift operation or for abrasive soil.

## Tools required

- Drain hose
- Insulated screwdriver set
- Vernier caliper or the wear gauge supplied with the machine
- Clean lint-free cloth

## Procedure

1. Switch the machine off and wait for the vacuum to stop completely.
2. Operate the filter shaker before opening the hopper, never after.
3. Release the hopper catches and withdraw the hopper far enough to reach the filter frame.
4. Lift the panel filter clear of its frame without knocking it against the housing.
5. Inspect the pleats against the light. A filter that passes no light through more than a quarter of its area is blinded and cleaning will not recover it.
6. Clean from the clean side outwards with low-pressure air, not from the dirty side.
7. Check the filter seal for compression set and replace it if it no longer springs back.
8. Refit the filter, making sure it seats squarely on the seal all the way round.
9. Close the hopper and confirm the seated indicator clears before returning to service.

## Limits and intervals

| Item | Limit | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squeegee blade | 3 mm wear | Check daily |
| Brush bristle | 12 mm minimum length | Measure every 50 h |
| Charging temperature | 40 C maximum | Before every charge |
| Filter seal | Replaces with filter | Every 250 h |

## Notes

- Any measurement taken outside the limits in the table is a reason to stop and raise a service request, not a reason to adjust the limit.
- Record the hour meter reading in the machine log when the procedure is complete. The next interval is counted from that reading, not from the calendar date.
