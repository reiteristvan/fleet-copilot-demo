# Service report — SD50B-2026-10005 — 2026-02-13

## Findings

Attended Hangar Seven for SD50B-2026-10005 at 1000 h. The panel filter was blinded across most of its area, which explains the loss of suction reported by M. Lindqvist.

## Work carried out

1. Recalibrated the deck height and confirmed even contact across the width.
2. Cleaned the float shut-off and the recovery tank breather, then flushed the suction path.
3. Advised the site on the charging temperature limit and left a copy of the procedure.

## Parts used

- Panel filter and seal
- Brush set, both sides

## Outcome

Returned to service. Recommended the site order a second set of blades to hold as spares.
