# Service report — SD50B-2026-10416 — 2026-01-25

## Findings

Found shrink wrap wound tightly around the brush drive shaft on SD50B-2026-10416. Bristle length measured within limits once the film was removed, so the overload was mechanical rather than wear.

## Work carried out

1. Cleaned the float shut-off and the recovery tank breather, then flushed the suction path.
2. Advised the site on the charging temperature limit and left a copy of the procedure.
3. Replaced the squeegee blade set and reset the deflection across the full width.

## Parts used

- Drive plate
- Float shut-off assembly

## Outcome

Returned to service with a note on the machine log to re-measure the brushes at the next interval.
