# Service report — SWR120-2026-10280 — 2026-09-10

## Findings

Inspection of SWR120-2026-10280 found the recovery tank float shut-off coated with detergent residue and sticking, which accounts for the intermittent E-134.

## Work carried out

1. Replaced the panel filter and its seal; the old seal had taken a compression set.
2. Recalibrated the deck height and confirmed even contact across the width.
3. Cleared the fault history after recording it, then ran the machine for twenty minutes.

## Parts used

- Panel filter and seal
- Drive plate

## Outcome

Fault not reproduced after the adjustment. Asked the site to log it again if it returns.
