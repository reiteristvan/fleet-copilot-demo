# Service report — VS75-2026-10141 — 2026-05-09

## Findings

Machine VS75-2026-10141 attended for repeated Deep discharge lockout. Battery pack surface temperature was still above ambient two hours after the shift ended, which points at the charging regime rather than at the pack.

## Work carried out

1. Cleaned the float shut-off and the recovery tank breather, then flushed the suction path.
2. Recalibrated the deck height and confirmed even contact across the width.
3. Replaced the squeegee blade set and reset the deflection across the full width.

## Parts used

- No parts used; adjustment only
- Squeegee blade set, item number 1.688-420.0

## Outcome

Returned to service. Recommended the site order a second set of blades to hold as spares.
