# Service report — SDM43-2026-10142 — 2026-02-16

## Findings

Machine SDM43-2026-10142 presented with Brush motor overload at 3830 h. Confirmed the fault on the floor; it did not reproduce with the deck raised.

## Work carried out

1. Cleared the debris from the brush drive and checked the shaft seal for damage.
2. Recalibrated the deck height and confirmed even contact across the width.
3. Updated the charger profile to the setting for the fitted pack chemistry.

## Parts used

- Brush set, both sides
- Squeegee blade set, item number 1.291-100.0

## Outcome

Returned to service. The underlying cause is operational rather than mechanical: the machine is being put on charge hot at the end of a double shift.
