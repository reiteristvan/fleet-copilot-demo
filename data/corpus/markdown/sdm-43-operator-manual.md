# Single-disc machine SDM-43 — operator manual

## Intended use

The Single-disc machine SDM-43 is intended for cleaning sealed hard floors indoors. It is not approved for outdoor use, for floors with a gradient above the value on the machine plate, or for picking up burning, explosive or toxic material.

## Configurations covered

| Item number | Battery | Deck | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.291-100.0 | AGM | pad | no tanks fitted |
| 1.291-101.0 | lithium-ion | brush | no tanks fitted |

## Controls

- The battery indicator shows the state of charge of the lithium-ion pack.
- The deck lever raises and lowers the brush deck. Raise it before reversing.
- The hour meter records running time and is the basis for every service interval.
- The horn is a legal requirement on the ride-on machines and must be tested daily.
- The key switch isolates the whole machine. Remove the key whenever the machine is unattended.

## Daily operation

1. Overlap each pass by about one tenth of the deck width.
2. Check the battery indicator shows enough charge for the whole run.
3. Walk the area and remove loose debris and obstacles before starting.
4. Select a speed that keeps the squeegee picking up cleanly; faster is not cleaner.
5. Lower the deck and open the solution tap.
6. Raise the deck and close the tap before stopping or reversing.

## Filling and emptying

1. Empty the recovery tank before it reaches full, not after the shut-off has tripped.
2. Add detergent at the dilution on the product label, into water, never the other way round.
3. Rinse the recovery tank at the end of every shift and leave the lid open to dry.

## After use

1. Wipe the squeegee blades and check them for damage.
2. Raise the squeegee off the floor so the blades do not take a set overnight.
3. Remove the brush or pad driver and rinse it.
4. Put the machine on charge and confirm the charger has started.
5. Drain and rinse both tanks.

## Cautions

- Never open the battery compartment while the charger is connected.
- Replace brushes at or below 12 mm bristle length. Past that point the deck carries load the bristles should take.
- Do not hose the machine down. Water entering the electrical compartment is not covered.
- Replace the squeegee blade once the wiping edge is worn back more than 3 mm. A worn blade leaves a wet floor, which is a slip hazard rather than a cosmetic problem.
