# Squeegee blade change — Walk-behind scrubber-dryer SD-50B

## Purpose

Applies to the Walk-behind scrubber-dryer SD-50B. The interval assumes single-shift use on a sealed floor; halve it for two-shift operation or for abrasive soil.

## Tools required

- 13 mm combination spanner
- Vernier caliper or the wear gauge supplied with the machine
- Torque wrench, 5-25 Nm
- Insulated screwdriver set

## Procedure

1. Park the machine on a level surface and switch it off at the key.
2. Raise the squeegee to its transport position and disconnect the suction hose.
3. Release the two wing nuts holding the squeegee body to the mounting bracket.
4. Withdraw the blade retaining strip and slide the blade clear of the body.
5. Inspect the wiping edge. Replace the blade if the edge is rounded, torn, or worn back more than 3 mm from its original profile.
6. Rotate the blade to an unused edge if three edges remain unworn, otherwise fit a new one.
7. Refit the retaining strip and tighten the wing nuts by hand only.
8. Reconnect the suction hose and lower the squeegee.
9. Run the machine over a wet patch and check the whole width lifts clean. A streak at one end means the deflection is uneven, not that the blade is worn.

## Limits and intervals

| Item | Limit | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squeegee blade | 3 mm wear | Check daily |
| Brush bristle | 12 mm minimum length | Measure every 50 h |
| Charging temperature | 40 C maximum | Before every charge |
| Filter seal | Replaces with filter | Every 250 h |

## Notes

- Dispose of used consumables according to the site waste procedure. Detergent-soaked material is not general waste.
- If a part is at or past its wear limit, replace it now rather than at the next interval. The limit is set where the consumable stops protecting the component behind it, and that component is the expensive one.
