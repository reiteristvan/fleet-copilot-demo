# Service report — SDM43-2026-10142 — 2026-02-03

## Findings

Inspection of SDM43-2026-10142 found the recovery tank float shut-off coated with detergent residue and sticking, which accounts for the intermittent E-041.

## Work carried out

1. Advised the site on the charging temperature limit and left a copy of the procedure.
2. Updated the charger profile to the setting for the fitted pack chemistry.
3. Replaced the panel filter and its seal; the old seal had taken a compression set.

## Parts used

- Float shut-off assembly
- Drive plate

## Outcome

Left out of service pending a replacement pack. The site has been told not to return it to the floor.
