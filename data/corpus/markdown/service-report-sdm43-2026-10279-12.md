# Service report — SDM43-2026-10279 — 2026-01-07

## Findings

Machine SDM43-2026-10279 attended for repeated Deep discharge lockout. Battery pack surface temperature was still above ambient two hours after the shift ended, which points at the charging regime rather than at the pack.

## Work carried out

1. Recalibrated the deck height and confirmed even contact across the width.
2. Cleared the fault history after recording it, then ran the machine for twenty minutes.
3. Replaced the panel filter and its seal; the old seal had taken a compression set.

## Parts used

- Squeegee blade set, item number 1.291-100.0
- Float shut-off assembly

## Outcome

Returned to service. The underlying cause is operational rather than mechanical: the machine is being put on charge hot at the end of a double shift.
