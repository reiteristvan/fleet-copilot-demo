# Service report — SDR90-2026-10416 — 2026-07-11

## Findings

Found shrink wrap wound tightly around the brush drive shaft on SDR90-2026-10416. Bristle length measured within limits once the film was removed, so the overload was mechanical rather than wear.

## Work carried out

1. Cleared the fault history after recording it, then ran the machine for twenty minutes.
2. Replaced the squeegee blade set and reset the deflection across the full width.
3. Advised the site on the charging temperature limit and left a copy of the procedure.

## Parts used

- Drive plate
- Squeegee blade set, item number 1.534-210.0

## Outcome

Machine returned to service. No recurrence during the twenty-minute test run.
