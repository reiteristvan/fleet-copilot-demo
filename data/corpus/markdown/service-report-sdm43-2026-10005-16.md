# Service report — SDM43-2026-10005 — 2026-05-10

## Findings

Called to Harbour Terminal. Reported symptom was poor water pickup on SDM43-2026-10005. Found the squeegee deflection uneven across the width and the left hand blade worn well past its limit.

## Work carried out

1. Advised the site on the charging temperature limit and left a copy of the procedure.
2. Cleared the debris from the brush drive and checked the shaft seal for damage.
3. Recalibrated the deck height and confirmed even contact across the width.

## Parts used

- Squeegee blade set, item number 1.291-101.0
- No parts used; adjustment only

## Outcome

Fault not reproduced after the adjustment. Asked the site to log it again if it returns.
